One identity, applied consistently.

These guidelines define how the Annorak brand looks, reads and behaves. The identity is dark-first and monochrome — closer to a modern software product than a traditional corporate brand. Surfaces are dark grey, never pure black; pure black and pure white are reserved for detail and maximum contrast. Follow the rules here for every internal and external asset.

01 / Brand Foundation

Precision engineering for the modern enterprise.

Annorak Intelligence Group designs data architecture, automation and business intelligence for mid-size and large companies. We replace manual, low-value work with engineered systems — AI, MCP agents, integrations and custom development — so teams decide and machines execute.

Precise

We lead with evidence and numbers, not adjectives.

Engineered

Structured, deliberate, built to last.

Restrained

Dark, quiet, monochrome. Nothing decorative.

Direct

Clear and affirmative, free of hype.

Mission
Engineer the systems that let modern enterprises run on precise, real-time intelligence instead of manual effort.
Vision
A standard where every core business process is measured, automated and continuously improved.

Analytical · understated · exacting · trustworthy.

02 / Logo

The mark

A custom wordmark with the triangular peak built into the "A" — an abstract anorak hood. The mark is monochrome: white is the primary treatment, used on our dark surfaces; black is the secondary treatment for light contexts. Never recoloured, re-typed or rebuilt.

Primary — on dark

White · on canvas #141414
White · on raised surface #222

Secondary — on light

Black · on white
Black · on light grey

Default to the white mark on dark. Use the black mark only when a light background is unavoidable (e.g. printed letterhead, third-party light UI).

03 / Tagline Lockup

The two-line signature

When the tagline accompanies the logo it is always set on two lines, in JetBrains Mono, uppercase, letter-spaced. It reads as a quiet technical signature beneath or beside the mark. The tagline never appears as a single inline string and never with a "//" prefix.

PrecisionEngineered To Scale
+ Correct — two lines, no slashes
// Precision, engineered to scale.
× Wrong — single line with "//"

Spacing: set the lockup tracking to ~0.30em. Keep the tagline subordinate to the mark — never larger or higher-contrast than the logo itself.

05 / Misuse

What never to do

Any alteration weakens the brand. When in doubt, use the master files unchanged on a dark surface.

Don't stretch or distort
Don't rotate or tilt the mark
Don't use low contrast
Don't add shadows or effects
// Precision, engineered to scale.
Don't add "//" or set tagline inline
Don't place on busy backgrounds
06 / Colour System

Dark-first. No colour.

The identity is monochrome and dark by default. Large surfaces use dark greys — never pure black. Pure black and pure white are reserved for fine detail and maximum-contrast moments (the logo, primary type). Hierarchy comes from elevation and contrast, not hue. Click any swatch to copy its HEX.

09 / Tone of Voice

Data over adjectives

Annorak writes like a senior engineer briefing a board: direct, technical, quantitative. Short affirmative sentences. State what a system does and what it delivers, then the number. Technical English terms only when standard (MCP, RAG, API, ETL, KPI, LLM).

Avoid — hollow marketing

transformempower revolutionarynext level synergiesgame-changing

Prefer — concrete & measured

reduces 60% of audit timepayback in ~1.2 months real-time, unified data10x ROI
Don't
"We empower organisations to transform their operations and unlock next-level synergies."
Do
"We automate the manual back-office, cutting processing time by 60% and freeing senior staff for higher-value work."

No em-dashes. No exclamation marks in headlines. No emojis. Result first, number second.

11 / Business Cards

Business cards

The card is the brand at its most concentrated: dark surface, white mark, monospace data. The front carries only the logo; the back carries the two-line signature, the person's details and the QR. Nothing else.

Front — logo centred on the dark surface. Shared across the team.
Back — signature top-left, contact block bottom-left, QR right. Personalised.

Format & print

  • Trim size 90 × 50 mm
  • Bleed 2 mm all sides
  • PDF page 94 × 54 mm
  • Sides double-sided
  • Files 1 front + 1 back/person

Colour & type

  • Surface #1E1E1E
  • Logo / name #EDEDED
  • Secondary text #999–#ABA
  • Name Inter Bold
  • Signature & data JetBrains Mono

Layout rules

  • Signature two lines
  • Accent bar left of contact
  • Top margin (slogan) = bottom margin
  • Side margins symmetric

QR placement

  • Top edge = name x-height
  • Bottom edge = web baseline
  • Right margin = slogan left margin
  • Quiet zone cropped to content

Construction principle: every element snaps to a shared margin. The QR mirrors the height of the contact block, and the slogan's top space equals the contact's bottom space — the card reads as one balanced grid, not parts placed by eye.
